# Customer interviews

## INT-001 — PixelForge
Segment: Small agency
Team size: 6
Role: Founder

"We signed up because I wanted the team to stop jumping between five different tools."
"The first client was pretty easy to add. Then it asked me to connect Meta and there were a lot of settings. I wasn't sure which account permissions it actually needed."
"I left it there because I had a client call. Came back two days later."
"We eventually connected it. Once the campaign was running, it was actually pretty convenient."

## INT-002 — Northstar Performance
Segment: Medium agency
Team size: 22
Role: Head of Performance

"We already use our own process for campaign setup."
"Connecting the advertising account wasn't difficult for me, but I've done this hundreds of times."
"What I care about is whether your reporting can replace the spreadsheet our team currently maintains."
"I don't need AI configuring campaigns for me. I'd rather have control."

## INT-003 — AdPilot
Segment: Small agency
Team size: 4
Role: Founder

"I created the workspace and then didn't really understand what I was supposed to do next."
"I clicked Connect Meta, saw the configuration screen and thought I'd finish it later."
"I wasn't sure if changing something there could affect the client's existing campaigns."
"If there was a safe demo or preview I probably would have continued."

## INT-004 — GrowthLab
Segment: Small agency
Team size: 9
Role: Performance Marketer

"Setup took longer than I expected."
"The annoying part wasn't logging into Meta. It was deciding what to select after that."
"I messaged your support because I didn't want to break anything in the client's account."
"Support sent me the recommended configuration and then I finished it pretty quickly."

## INT-005 — ScaleWorks
Segment: Medium agency
Team size: 27
Role: Operations Manager

"Our onboarding was fine."
"The bigger issue for us is inviting the rest of the team and defining who can see which clients."
"We manage around 40 client accounts, so permissions are much more important than setup."

## INT-006 — RocketGrowth
Segment: Small agency
Team size: 7
Role: Founder

"I connected Google Ads but never launched anything."
"I wasn't sure whether I needed to recreate the client's campaign inside GrowthOS or import the existing one."
"At that point I just went back to Google Ads because I had work to do."
"The product looked promising, I just didn't have time to learn a new workflow."
