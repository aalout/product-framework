# GrowthOS

## Product

GrowthOS is a B2B SaaS platform for small and medium performance-marketing agencies.

Agencies use GrowthOS to manage advertising campaigns for their clients:
- create client workspaces;
- connect advertising accounts;
- configure campaigns;
- launch campaigns;
- monitor performance;
- run marketing experiments.

## Target users

Primary segment:
Small and medium performance-marketing agencies with 3–30 employees.

Primary user:
Performance marketer / agency owner responsible for launching campaigns.

## Core value proposition

Launch and iterate client advertising campaigns faster with less manual operational work.

## Current product stage

Early product.
First paying customers are using the platform.
The team is still validating core workflows and activation mechanics.

## Current business goal

Increase activation of newly registered agencies.

## Activation definition

An account is considered activated when it launches its first advertising campaign.

## Current onboarding

1. User creates an account.
2. Creates first client workspace.
3. Connects an advertising account.
4. Configures advertising integration.
5. Creates first campaign.
6. Launches campaign.

## Product principles

- Optimize for time-to-value.
- Prefer validating risky assumptions before building expensive solutions.
- Product decisions should be evidence-driven.
- AI-generated recommendations are not automatically accepted as product decisions.
- Product and engineering decisions must remain traceable to evidence.

## Current constraints

- Engineering team is small.
- Avoid large infrastructure investments before validating demand.
- Enterprise customers are not currently the primary segment.
